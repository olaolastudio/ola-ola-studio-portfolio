Project Info

Windmill makes smart, design-forward air conditioners for modern homes. In this spec campaign — developed in collaboration with copywriter Laura Ornella — I led art direction across OOH, digital ads, social content, and a brand collaboration with Brooklinen and The New York Times.

The creative strategy pushed back on a familiar industry habit: leading with awards and superlatives that tell consumers nothing about whether the AC will actually make their summer bearable, their kitchen usable, or their roommate tolerable. Instead, we centered the work on real people, real moments, and one simple promise — a cool place to hang.
