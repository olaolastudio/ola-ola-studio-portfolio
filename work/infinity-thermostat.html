Project Info

Infinity is a smart home thermostat designed for modern living. As part of an in-house design team, I concepted and presented the visual direction for the product ecosystem — designing across the mobile app, the wall-mounted device interface, and a brand style guide covering light and dark modes.

Services

Product Design, User Experience, Interface Design

Credits

  • Ana Lopez — Product Design
  • Tae Yang — Product Design
  • Jill Haefele — Interaction Design, Research
  • Micah Lindenberger — Art Direction

The redesign of the Infinity homeowner application created an opportunity to establish a more cohesive identity and portray itself as a modern, high-end, and intelligent product line within the Carrier brand family.

One of our main goals was to create clean layouts to help users navigate through the app and easily scan content and configure their preferences.
