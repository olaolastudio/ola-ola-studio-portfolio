Project Info

Chanel is a legacy luxury beauty brand navigating an increasingly digital customer experience.

Identifying a gap in their mobile offering, I designed a concept app that explores what it looks like when a heritage luxury brand translates its identity into a seamless, shoppable mobile experience — staying faithful to Chanel's aesthetic while meeting the expectations of a modern user.
