Project Info

Farm River Farm in New Haven, Connecticut was the setting for "Balling the Queen," a two-night immersive dinner series exploring honeybees, consumption, and collapse.

Organized by curator Caroline Maxwell and chef Jen Monroe, the event was written about in Vogue. I was brought in as photographer for both evenings, capturing the event as it unfolded across the farm, forest, and table, with images commissioned to accompany the feature.
