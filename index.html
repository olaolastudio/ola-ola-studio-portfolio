About Ana

Ola Ola Studio is the creative practice of Ana Lopez, an art director and designer based in New York.

She partners with businesses and brands looking for a strong creative vision — to sharpen their identity and develop a visual language that's distinctly their own. Her services include brand identity, art direction, graphic design, and photography, with a particular focus on beauty, lifestyle, fashion, and consumer technology.

She brings confident creative expertise, strong art direction, and the communication skills to move a vision from concept to execution.
